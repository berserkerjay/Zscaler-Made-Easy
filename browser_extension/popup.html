This site

Opens the Path analyzer with this site filled in: what Zscaler does to a user's traffic to it, hop by hop. The address is sent without its query string. Nothing runs until you click Analyze there.

Badges on DLP dictionaries, engines and rules in the ZIA admin console, what uses them and their findings, and export as JSON or Terraform. Switch the badges on in settings.

Start Zscaler Made Easy (START_Zscaler_Made_Easy, ./start.sh or the container), or set its address in settings.
