Zscaler Made Easy companion

The address of the Zscaler Made Easy app on this computer. A local install listens on http://127.0.0.1:8000; the container on http://127.0.0.1:8001. Only addresses on this computer (127.0.0.1, localhost) are accepted.

Badges in the Zscaler admin console

Shows a Zscaler Made Easy badge next to DLP dictionaries, engines and rules on the ZIA admin console (admin.zscaler.net and the other Zscaler clouds): what uses them, or their most serious finding. Your browser asks you to allow the extension on those sites. The badges compare names on the page with what Zscaler Made Easy read, inside the page: nothing from the console is sent anywhere.

The extension stores only this address. It holds no credentials or tenant data: signing in, the UI token, write mode and every tenant read stay in the app.
