How a deployment works

Every policy pack takes the same road into a tenant and the same road out, whatever the pack is called. Click a step to see what happens there, what can stop it, and what you would see on screen. Nothing on this page touches your tenant.

    Back to the app
    One road for every pack. The Fed policy pack was the first deployed in production; its way of doing things (dictionaries, then engines, then rules; one rule per engine; every rule created switched off) is now the only way, applied by one piece of code to every pack. A test fails the build if anything tries to go around it.
    Nothing is ever written without a plan you approved. The plan is read again just before writing; if the tenant changed in between, nothing is written and you see the new plan.
    If something fails, the message says what and why. Each object that could not be created or removed shows ZIA's own answer and, for every answer the app knows, a plain sentence on what it means and what to do.
