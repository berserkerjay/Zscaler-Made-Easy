Change queue

Changes someone prepared but did not apply: a policy-pack deploy plan, a selective removal plan or a fix recipe, filed with Request approval in Deploy policy packs or Review & remediate. A writer other than the requester approves or rejects each one (four-eyes). An approved request is applied from here: the plan is checked against the tenant again first, and a request whose plan no longer matches is marked stale and nothing is written. Every step is in the audit log with who did it.
