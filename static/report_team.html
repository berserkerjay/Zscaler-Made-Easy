Who may use this server and what each person may do on each tenant profile. A role is the most a person may ever do (viewer reads, operator plans and applies, approver approves someone else's change, administrator does everything and manages this page); a grant says on which tenant profiles they may do it. Nothing is allowed until it is granted.
