Log analyzer for Client Connector logs and PAC files

Drop in the ZIP that Client Connector's Export Logs produced (or single .log files, or a PAC file). You get back what happened, what it means for the user, and what to do next, in plain English. Nothing leaves this computer and nothing is stored.

1. Choose the files

How to get the bundle: on the user's device open the Zscaler tray icon → More → Export Logs. It saves a ZIP; drop that ZIP here as it is. You can also drop the files in this box.
