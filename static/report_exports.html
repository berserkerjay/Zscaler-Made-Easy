Zscaler IP ranges and allowlists

For the firewall in front of Client Connector, Branch and App Connectors or tunnels: Zscaler's published ranges and host names for one cloud, aggregated per region and in all, with ranges you leave out or add, as a plain list, CSV, Palo Alto external dynamic lists, JSON or a Microsoft 365-style list. Nothing is sent to any firewall: you download the file. This does not read your tenant.

The ranges come from Zscaler's config portal, config.zscaler.com. Fetching it is off until you turn it on here, and then happens only when you click Fetch now; nothing about your tenant is sent. Without internet access, save each feed's JSON from the address shown on another machine and import it. Every feed is checked: private, reserved or over-broad ranges and host names outside Zscaler's domains are dropped and counted. A feed not fetched or imported is unknown, never empty; one older than 30 days is marked stale.
