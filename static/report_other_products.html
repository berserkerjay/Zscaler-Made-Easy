Other Zscaler products

This page reads the Zscaler products beyond ZIA, ZPA and Client Connector: Zero Trust Branch, External attack surface (EASM), Business Insights, Zero Trust Cellular and Microsegmentation. For each one you ticked on the Scope page it lists what the tenant has, explains it in plain words and checks it against best practice. Click Read, then fix the high findings first. Nothing is changed in your Zscaler.

← Back to the assessment Excel JSON
How it works
  1. Tick what to read on the Scope page. Each product has its own box, off by default: Zero Trust Branch, External attack surface (EASM), Business Insights, Zero Trust Cellular and Microsegmentation. Nothing else is read. They need a OneAPI API client; Zero Trust Cellular also needs its customer ID (above), and Microsegmentation uses the ZPA customer ID unless you give another.
  2. Read. The page reads once, shown live as it reads. Stop ends the read at once. A product the tenant does not own says "not licensed" and is not judged.
  3. Fix the findings, high first: each says what is wrong, why it matters, what to change and where the advice comes from. Export them as Excel or JSON for the change request.
Never shown: API keys, provisioning keys, secrets or tokens, the people a report or an alert goes to, and any device or SIM (they are counted).
