## How the score is computed

Each sub-area has three parts, each from 0 to 100:

- **Tests** (40%): passed / (passed + failed) of the sub-area's test files in the recorded run (skipped tests are left out; Regression uses the whole suite).
- **Findings** (40%): 100 minus the penalty of each open finding in the sub-area's records, never below 0. Fixed and accepted findings are listed but not penalised.
- **Coverage** (20%): required standards controls with at least one mapped test (OWASP ASVS 4.0.3 L2, CWE, WCAG 2.2 AA, SLSA v1.0, OpenSSF Scorecard, NIST SSDF).

A part with no input (no record, no matching tests) is left out and the other weights are scaled up; the table says what each score is based on. An area is the mean of its sub-areas; the overall score is the weighted mean of the areas, rounded.

| Severity (CVSS v3.1) | Base score band | Penalty per open finding | Cap on the overall score |
|---|---|---|---|
| critical | 9.0-10.0 | 40 | 39 |
| high | 7.0-8.9 | 20 | 69 |
| medium | 4.0-6.9 | 8 | — |
| low | 0.1-3.9 | 2 | — |
| info | 0.0 | 0 | — |

| Area | Weight | Sub-areas |
|---|---|---|
| Regression | 25 | Regression |
| Secure by design | 35 | Write paths, Untrusted input, Auth, scope and secrets, Supply chain |
| Functionality | 25 | Traffic analysis, Builders, UI and accessibility |
| Motion | 15 | Motion |

| Score | Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 90 or more | A (Strong) | Tests pass, no serious finding is open and the standards are well covered. |
| 80 or more | B (Good) | Mostly sound: a few gaps or minor open findings. |
| 70 or more | C (Fair) | Usable, with gaps worth closing soon. |
| 60 or more | D (Weak) | Serious gaps: an open high finding, failing tests or thin coverage. |
| 0 or more | F (Poor) | Not acceptable: an open critical finding or many failures. |

Hard rule: any open critical finding caps the overall score at 39 and any open high finding at 69, whatever the other parts say; the score line names the findings that caused it. There is no overall score without a recorded full test run.
